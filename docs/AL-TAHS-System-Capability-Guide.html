AL-TAHS System Capability and Role Guide

This page gives a practical overview of what the AL-TAHS platform does, who can do what, and the operational responsibilities for each user role.

Manual reference page for onboarding and daily operations.

1) What The System Is Capable Of

AL-TAHS is an integrated business operations platform for sales, stock, HR, reporting, and motorbike branch operations.

Sales and POS

  • Invoice creation, returns, EBM confirmation, shift open/close, and shift export.
  • POS search with branch/location-aware availability checks.
  • Sales tracking synchronized with reports and audit trail.

Stock and Inventory

  • Products, locations, bins, stock-in/out/damage, and adjustments.
  • Stock movement visibility and valuation for decision making.
  • Low stock and lifecycle visibility for management control.

HR Operations

  • Employee records, attendance, salary advances, and payroll runs.
  • Payroll generation, deduction handling, and bank export.
  • HR summaries for daily and monthly administration.

Management and Governance

  • Manager/CEO dashboards, KPIs, EBM pending, and audit viewer.
  • Expense management and financial summaries.
  • System user administration with role-based access control.

Motorbike and Branch Flow

  • Motorbike branch assignment and branch-level sales handling.
  • Promotion records, search/sort, and imported historical data continuity.
  • Salesperson workflow linked to branch reporting.

Data Integrity and Operations

  • Role-restricted actions to reduce accidental misuse.
  • Error logging for maintenance and troubleshooting.
  • Backup-first operations for business continuity.

4) Role-by-Role Practical Guide

CEO

Review overall dashboard health daily, validate high-risk actions, and enforce data discipline across teams.

Manager

Track KPIs, pending EBM records, audit anomalies, and expense behavior; coordinate fixes immediately.

HR

Keep employee master data current, mark attendance on time, and complete payroll with verified deductions.

Cashier

Open shift, process sales accurately, confirm EBM, and close shift with reconciled outputs and receipts.

Store Keeper

Ensure every movement has a valid location/bin context, and maintain stock correctness for all sellable items.

Salesperson

Manage branch motorbike lifecycle, update sold status from reports, and keep promotion/sales data synchronized.

5) Usage Rules To Keep Data Clean

  • Never share accounts; each user must log in with their own role.
  • Cashier must close shift daily and confirm EBM status for completed transactions.
  • Store operations must always map stock to valid location/bin where required.
  • HR payroll should be finalized only after attendance and advances are reviewed.
  • Manager/CEO must review audit and pending dashboards at least once per day.
Recommended daily routine: verify service health, process operations by role, close the day with reports, and ensure automated backups complete successfully.

6) Quick Start For New Staff

  1. Login with assigned role account and change password on first login.
  2. Open only the menu items relevant to your role and follow role-specific process.
  3. Complete records before moving to next step (sale, stock entry, attendance, payroll action, etc.).
  4. If access is denied or data looks wrong, report to Manager/CEO instead of bypassing workflow.
